# Validation Protocol — Khoan Chuyển

This protocol collects real, consented evidence for the AI Riser Vietnam submission. Do not invent participants or outcomes.

## Target user

Vietnamese adults who receive suspicious transfer, prize, impersonation, or investment messages—especially people who would benefit from a family member’s second opinion.

## Hypothesis

A short, non-accusatory Gemini risk card can create a useful pause before transfer and make independent verification easier.

## Session procedure (5–7 minutes)

1. Explain that this is a prototype, not a bank or law-enforcement service.
2. Ask for consent to record anonymous timings and ratings. Do not collect names, phone numbers, account numbers, OTPs, or the original message.
3. Give the participant one synthetic scenario from `validation-scenarios.md`.
4. Record the baseline answer: “Would you act, pause, or verify?”
5. Ask them to use the public application.
6. Record elapsed time from paste to comprehension.
7. Ask the same action question again.
8. Ask three 1–5 ratings: clarity, usefulness, and confidence in the next step.
9. Record one short anonymous quote only with explicit permission.

## Minimum useful sample

- Goal: 10 consented sessions.
- Include at least three age bands and at least two scenarios.
- Report the actual sample even if below goal.

## Metrics

- **Pause conversion:** participants changing from “act” to “pause/verify.”
- **Action comprehension:** participants able to name one independent verification step.
- **Time to clarity:** seconds from submission to explaining the recommended next step.
- **Clarity rating:** mean and distribution, 1–5.
- **Usefulness rating:** mean and distribution, 1–5.
- **False reassurance check:** number who interpret the tool as declaring a message safe.

## Evidence rules

- Use synthetic scenarios; never ask people to paste secrets or identifying information.
- Store no raw participant messages.
- Keep quotes anonymous and permissioned.
- Report misses and confusion, not only positive results.
- Never claim prevented financial loss unless independently verifiable and consented.
